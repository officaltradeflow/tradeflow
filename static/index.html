🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. What We Collect

We collect your username, email address, and hashed (encrypted) password when you register. We also store your virtual trade history and portfolio data for the purpose of running the simulation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate TradeFlow. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Email addresses are used only for account recovery.

3. Data Security

All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — we never store plain-text passwords. All connections are encrypted via HTTPS. hCaptcha is used to protect forms from automated abuse.

4. Cookies & Storage

We use localStorage to store your session token on your device. No advertising cookies are used. hCaptcha may set cookies governed by their own privacy policy.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, please contact the site administrator.
